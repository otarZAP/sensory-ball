Why power directly from the LiPo (3.7 V) for the loads instead of 5 V: the XIAO's 5V pin only sources 5 V when USB is connected. Running off battery would require a boost converter (MT3608 or similar) to get 5 V to the NeoPixel ring and DFPlayer. Both parts are spec'd to run from 3.7 V — slightly dim LEDs and slightly quieter audio — which is acceptable here and saves ~10 mm² of board area and ~5 % of battery life. If a future revision wants full brightness, drop in a small boost on the LED rail only.

Pin name vs GPIO number gotcha: the XIAO silkscreen shows logical pins (D0–D10), but in code you can use either the silkscreen names (e.g. D0) or the raw ESP32-S3 GPIO numbers (e.g. GPIO1). When in doubt, use the silkscreen names — they're stable across XIAO variants. The GPIO column is here for cross-referencing the ESP32-S3 datasheet only.
